# PMF / Narrative / Execution rubric (score each 1-5; justify with claim ids)

**Product-Market Fit**: is there evidence that a specific user wants this now?
- 5: paying or transacting usage that is growing, from users who are not the team (on-chain volume, customers).
- 3: real developer adoption (forks, integrations, outside contributors) but no proof of payment.
- 1: a repo or landing page with no outside usage evidence.

**Narrative**: is the story clear, timely and fundable in the thesis (AI agents x payments / fintech)?
- 5: a crisp wedge tied to a shift that is happening now (e.g. agents paying per API call over x402), with a
  credible path from wedge to platform.
- 3: in the right space but generic ("payments for AI") or crowded without a stated edge.
- 1: unclear what it is, or the story depends on a shift with no evidence.

**Execution**: does the team ship?
- 5: sustained, recent shipping velocity (commits, releases, docs) by more than one person, and fast follow-up
  on standards changes.
- 3: steady but slow or single-maintainer.
- 1: stale, abandoned or archived.

Scores are relative to pre-seed / seed companies, not to incumbents. Evidence beats adjectives.
