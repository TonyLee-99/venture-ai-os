Connect

Mandate
A signed spend authority: cap, scope, expiry. The model can't rewrite it.

Record

The problem
Money is the last thing agents can't move.
Money out, stuck
The agent compares four offers, picks the best one, and stops. Someone copies a boleto line into a bank app and loses the afternoon. The supplier waits. The deal waits.
Money in, lost
A customer's agent is ready to buy and your checkout asks for a human. A calling agent would pay for your API and you give it away or turn it down.
