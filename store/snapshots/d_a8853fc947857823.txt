DeFi  ·  Prediction markets
ThermoMarket
ThermoMarket needed order processing and live market data to handle concentrated activity around breaking news. We separated off-chain matching from on-chain settlement, gave each market a single sequenced writer, recorded accepted orders in a durable command journal and moved live prices onto a dedicated WebSocket fleet. Users keep their own keys, and Signal Orders can trigger a trade from another market.
