What we believe
Three ideas behind the product.
Agents will pay where money already moves
Instant payment methods such as Pix made fast, programmable payments a daily habit. Agents will use them too.
Control is the product
Moving money is solved. Deciding whether an agent may move it, and proving what happened, is the work.
Rules belong outside the model
The model prepares the intent. Mandates, limits and review run on the server.
